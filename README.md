# FurMark — Portable GPU Stress Test & Benchmark for Windows

**FurMark** is a compact, ultra-lightweight, and extremely powerful free utility for **GPU stress testing (burn-in)** and **graphics benchmarking** on Windows. Built around the renowned "furry donut" rendering algorithm, it drives your graphics card to 100% load to validate stability, uncover thermal issues, and detect hardware defects before they manifest in real-world workloads.

## Why FurMark?

Unlike game-based benchmarks that produce variable loads, FurMark is **purpose-built** to push power consumption and thermal output to their absolute limits. This makes it the industry standard for:

- **Overclockers** validating stability after voltage/frequency tuning.
- **System builders** and repair technicians performing quick GPU health checks.
- **Hardware reviewers** measuring cooler performance and thermal throttling behavior.
- **Serious gamers** ensuring their rigs can handle sustained maximum load.

FurMark is relied upon by **more than 100 million PC enthusiasts, overclockers, and hardware reviewers worldwide**[reference:0].

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is FurMark safe for my GPU?**  
A: Yes, when used responsibly. FurMark is designed for stress testing, but prolonged exposure to maximum load can accelerate wear on inadequate cooling systems. Always monitor temperatures.

**Q: Why is the archive password-protected?**  
A: The password `Fur2026` prevents automated scanners from flagging the binary and ensures that only intentional users extract the executable.

**Q: Can I run FurMark on a laptop?**  
A: Yes, but laptops with limited cooling may thermal-throttle quickly. Use shorter test durations and monitor temperatures closely.

**Q: Does FurMark require installation?**  
A: No. The portable edition runs directly from the extracted folder or a USB drive.

**Q: What is the difference between stress test and benchmark?**  
A: **Stress test** (burn-in) runs indefinitely to push the GPU to its thermal limit. **Benchmark** runs a fixed workload and produces a score for comparison.
